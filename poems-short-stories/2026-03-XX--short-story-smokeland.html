The Bumpy Road of History Was Trending to Good

Out on the plains, standing upright, the Walkers started their day. Dad taps Aunt, who's cat-napping on her feet, then rounds up Daughter, Son, Brother-Son and New-Wife. They check their satchels and check for the ground for dropped stuff. With a bird-call to the right direction in the distance, they start their daily wander with the other family nearby.

On the Northern Seas, the long-boat Captain checks his wayfinding stone. His niece, the Rudder-Steer agrees - they won't make it back home after barely escaping that storm, with the fish-barrels they have left. They tell the crew, they all heave-to, and many nights later make their landing. After restocking their barrels with local stock, they head back and pray to find gold-wearing lords while they pillage and rob.

On the green-and-brown fields, a family of farmers begins their work. Check the map, and scout the next section for mines. The bodies can be looted for guns, bullets, knives and more if they had a backpack. In the distance, they can see a team recovering another tank. Picking out good parts and bad, they'll be able to make a working tractor, which will help all the nearby farmers plow fields, harvest grain - BANG - Hopefully that wasn't five funerals at once. The tractor would help speed things up, but they've still got horses, and people are what matter most.

The Comedy Pit

The Comedian does his skit, getting a few boos but more cheers. He's been here a while, so he's comfortable pushing the boundaries - feces, urine, sex, drugs - there's not a lot he can't joke about. Every few weeks, he even puts on a wig and a skirt, to make sure the quota's filled for women's humor.

The Jokester takes over, doing a milder routine. He's younger and still nervous about meeting quota while not transgressing into any material that will land him in jail. He even does a couple meta-jokes about how hard it is to know what jokes are illegal and what are allowed. That gets a few raised eyebrows and awkward coughs, but The Comedian waves him on - If it was actually illegal, the building would have been raided already.

As the night goes on, the laughs come and go, and there's even a few women in the crowd. (Dressed in their long-skirts and head-scarves, as is mandated.) Eventually there's a standing ovation, although as it starts the guy on stage is somewhat confused - it wasn't a particularly good joke. Still, he waves and accepts the praise, encouraging the audience. That is, until he realizes nobody else has started standing up. It's just one person - The Trench-Coat.

His mind races as he dives to the floor of the stage. His vision motion-blurs just as they shoot message-canisters into the corners of the room. (Those will be found later, with their manifestos and demands.) The Trench-Coat presses the button in their hand, and turns thirty paces into a fine rest mist. Most of the rest of the audience dies from the shock-wave or the ball-bearings and nails, but only a few people live.

The Comedian is one of them - stumbling to his feet, dazed and with ears ringing. If he'd have known this was going to happen tonight, he'd have called in sick, and gotten one of his juniors to take over. He starts thinking about what type of surgery he'll need, since his leg feels busted, and his hearing was already starting to suffer.

Then he finds a pistol beside one of the accomplices, and picks it up. He's notes all the events of the evening that he'll explain to the cops, when he gives a chuckle at the situation. It's time for one of his juniors to take over this corpolitic apologia - he makes his decision, and ends his career.
