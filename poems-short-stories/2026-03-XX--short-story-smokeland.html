The Comedy Pit

The Comedian does his skit, getting a few boos but more cheers. He's been here a while, so he's comfortable pushing the boundaries - feces, urine, sex, drugs - there's not a lot he can't joke about. Every few weeks, he even puts on a wig and a skirt, to make sure the quota's filled for women's humor.

The Jokester takes over, doing a milder routine. He's younger and still nervous about meeting quota while not transgressing into any material that will land him in jail. He even does a couple meta-jokes about how hard it is to know what jokes are illegal and what are allowed. That gets a few raised eyebrows and awkward coughs, but The Comedian waves him on - If it was actually illegal, the building would have been raided already.

As the night goes on, the laughs come and go, and there's even a few women in the crowd. (Dressed in their long-skirts and head-scarves, as is mandated.) Eventually there's a standing ovation, although as it starts the guy on stage is somewhat confused - it wasn't a particularly good joke. Still, he waves and accepts the praise, encouraging the audience. That is, until he realizes it was started by The Trench-Coat.

He dives to the floor of the stage, just as they shoot message-canisters into the corners of the room. (Those will be found later, with their manifestos and demands.) The Trench-Coat presses the button in their hand, turning into red mist along with all the people immediately beside them. Most of the rest of the audience dies from the shock-wave or the ball-bearings and nails, but only a few people live.

The Comedian is one of them - stumbling to his feet, dazed and with ears ringing. If he'd have known this was going to happen tonight, he'd have called in sick, and gotten one of his juniors to take over. He starts thinking about what type of surgery he'll need, since his leg feels busted, and his hearing was already starting to suffer.

Then he finds a pistol beside on of the accomplices, and picks it up. He's noting all the events of the evening that he'll explain to the cops, when a coin flips in his head. It's time for one of his juniors to take over this corpolitic apologia. He pulls back the hammer, and ends his career.
